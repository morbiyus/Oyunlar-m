About Surgeon Simulator AZE

A browser-based surgical trauma simulator inspired by Trauma Lab. You are the surgeon. A patient arrives on the table with multiple injuries — bleeding wounds, fractures, organ damage, even cardiac arrest. Your job is to keep their vitals stable and treat every injury before time runs out.

  • Vitals monitor: heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen, blood volume
  • X-ray mode: see bones, organs, and vessels beneath the skin
  • 10+ surgical tools: scalpel, forceps, suction, sutures, tourniquet, defibrillator, bone saw, clamps
  • Simple & Complex surgery: one-click treatment or full multi-step procedures
  • Procedural patients: every case is different
  • Works on desktop & mobile: touch-friendly controls
Controls
Click a tool to select it, then click the patient to use it.
X X-ray • Space Pause • Tab Tool menu • Drag / WASD pan camera • Scroll to zoom
